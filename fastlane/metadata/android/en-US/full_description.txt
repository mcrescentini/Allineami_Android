Allineami is a timer for people wearing clear dental aligners.

Orthodontists usually ask you to wear aligners 22 hours a day, leaving 2 hours for eating, brushing your teeth and cleaning them. Allineami keeps count for you: tap "Togli Allineatore" when you take them out and "Monta Allineatore" when you put them back in.

MY STORY
I went through a clear aligner treatment and it wasn't always easy to remember to put my aligners back in after meals. So I built Allineami to help myself: thanks to its reminders I made it to the end of my treatment. Now I'm offering it for free to anyone on the same journey.

FEATURES
• Ring showing the time you have left: green, orange or red, with an "OVER" warning
• Daily summary: hours worn, hours out, current session
• Chronometer always visible in your notifications while aligners are out
• Reminders every 15 minutes while your aligners are out
• Weekly statistics with a chart against your goal
• Edit or delete past days
• CSV export to show your orthodontist at check-ups
• Adjustable goal from 10 to 23 hours a day

PRIVACY
No account, no internet connection, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your phone only.

OPEN SOURCE
The source code is public under the GPL-3.0 license.

The app's interface is in Italian. Allineami is a reminder tool, not a medical device: always follow your orthodontist's instructions.
